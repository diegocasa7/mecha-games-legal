Endless Words

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Overview

Endless Words is a mobile game developed by Mecha Games. The core gameplay can be played without creating an account. The application includes advertising and optional in-app purchases.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, shared, and processed when you use Endless Words.

Accounts and Personal Information

Endless Words does not provide user accounts or sign-in functionality. Mecha Games does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or other sensitive personal information through the application.

Information Collected and Shared

Endless Words uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and optional rewarded advertisements. This service may automatically collect and share the following categories of information:

  • Approximate location: an approximate general location may be inferred from the device's IP address.
  • Application interactions: information such as application launches, taps, and interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostics: information about the performance, stability, launch time, energy usage, errors, or operation of the application and advertising SDK.
  • Device or other identifiers: identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device or account-related identifiers.

This information may be collected and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Information transmitted by Google services is encrypted in transit using secure network protocols.

Advertising

Endless Words displays banner advertisements and may offer optional rewarded advertisements that provide an in-game benefit, such as an extra life.

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can learn how Google processes information through its advertising services by reviewing How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Endless Words uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required. Available options may depend on your country or region.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android device settings. Restricting advertising identifiers or choosing non-personalized advertisements does not necessarily prevent all advertising, diagnostics, security, or basic measurement data from being processed.

Additional advertising controls are available through Google My Ad Center.

In-App Purchases

Endless Words offers optional purchases through Google Play Billing, including removal of banner advertisements and consumable extra lives.

Purchases and payments are processed by Google Play. Mecha Games does not receive or store your complete payment card or bank account information. The application may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and transaction tokens needed to provide, consume, or restore purchased content and to help prevent fraud.

Google's handling of payment and account information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Stored on Your Device

Gameplay information, such as your score, best score, premium status, and application preferences, may be stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide game functionality and remember your settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the application's storage through Android settings or uninstall Endless Words.

Data processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable policies, user controls, and legal obligations. Because Endless Words does not create user accounts or operate its own remote user database, Mecha Games does not provide a separate in-app account deletion mechanism.

Children's Privacy

Endless Words does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and related information may be processed by Google as described in this policy, subject to applicable consent, age-related, and advertising requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information directly to Mecha Games may contact us using the address below.

Third-Party Services

Endless Words uses the following Google services:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google AdMob;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP); and
  • Google Play Billing.

These services may process information according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
