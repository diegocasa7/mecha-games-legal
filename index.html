Endless Words

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

Overview

Endless Words is a free, offline mobile game developed by Mecha Games. Your privacy is important to us, and the application is designed to work without collecting personal information.

Information We Collect

Endless Words does not collect, access, transmit, sell, or share personal information or sensitive user data.

The application does not include:

  • user accounts or sign-in;
  • advertising;
  • analytics or tracking;
  • cloud services or developer-operated servers; or
  • third-party services that collect data through the application.

Data Stored on Your Device

Gameplay data, including your score, best score, and application preferences, is stored only on your device. This local data is used solely to provide the game's functionality and remember your settings. Mecha Games cannot access it, and the application does not transmit it elsewhere.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the application's data through Android settings or uninstall Endless Words. Either action deletes the locally stored score, best score, and preferences.

Children's Privacy

Endless Words does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. Because no personal information is collected, the application does not use personal information for profiling, advertising, or any other purpose.

Changes to This Policy

If the application's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are released. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Endless Words, contact Mecha Games at anarqui.contacto@gmail.com.
